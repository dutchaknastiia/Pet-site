Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your dog's stay

What vaccinations are required?

All dogs must be up-to-date with standard core vaccinations (Rabies, DHPP) and treated for fleas and ticks at least 3 days prior to check-in. Veterinary passport is required.

What should I bring with us?

Please bring your dog's regular food (to avoid stomach upset), favorite toy, bedding/bed, and leash. We provide bowls, water, and plenty of love!

What happens if my dog gets homesick or sad?

Our experienced sitters give extra cuddles, engage in favorite games, and offer high-value treats to keep spirits high. We also immediately contact you if any unusual behavior persists.

Can I visit before booking?

Yes, absolutely! We welcome introductory visits so you can inspect our premises, meet Marina, and let your dog get familiar with the environment.
